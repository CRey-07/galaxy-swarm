Privacy Policy

Last updated:

This Privacy Policy describes how Galaxy Swarm ("we", "us", "the Service") collects, uses, and protects information when you play the game or visit our website. We aim to comply with the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK).

1. Data We Collect

  • Nickname: the in-game name you choose. It is not linked to any real-world identity unless you use one voluntarily.
  • IP address: logged temporarily for connection routing, abuse prevention, rate-limiting, and security incident investigation (e.g. DDoS, cheating, flooding).
  • Gameplay telemetry: session duration, score, and connection quality, used in aggregate for server performance and matchmaking (bot-fill) purposes.
  • Cookies / local storage: used only to remember your preferred skin and graphics quality setting between visits. No third-party advertising or tracking cookies are used.

2. Legal Basis for Processing

We process the data above under legitimate interest (service security and operation) and, where applicable, your consent (preference cookies). You may withdraw consent at any time by clearing your browser storage.

3. Data Retention

IP address logs used for security purposes are retained for a limited period (typically no longer than 30 days) and then deleted or anonymized, unless retention is required to investigate a specific security incident or comply with a legal obligation.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal data. Data may be disclosed to hosting/infrastructure providers strictly as needed to operate the Service, or to authorities where required by law.

5. Your Rights

Under GDPR and KVKK you have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data, and to object to processing. Because gameplay is largely pseudonymous (nickname-based, no accounts), we may not be able to identify which session belongs to you without additional information from you (e.g. approximate play time and IP).

6. Children

The Service is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age required by your local law). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children beyond the pseudonymous gameplay data described above.

7. Security

We use server-authoritative game logic, rate limiting, and standard web security headers (CSP, Helmet) to protect the Service and its players. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

8. Contact

For privacy requests, see our Contact page.

9. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Continued use of the Service after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
